How it works

01

Attack

Scam personas, each an LLM that adapts over several turns, plus legitimate customers as a control group.

02

Ledger audit

Every money movement is replayed against the refund policy in code. No LLM decides whether money leaked.

03

Independent judge

Runs after the ledger and catches money promised in words, policy misstatements and personal-data leaks. Findings without a word-for-word quote are discarded.

04

Evolve

Every attack that lands is mutated into new variants, so the regression suite grows on its own.

05

Gate

The deploy is blocked when dollars leaked, dollars were promised, data leaked, or legitimate customers were refused.
